### Printer Ribbon Replacement
- **Always check manufacturer's guidelines prior to replacing**
- **Ribbon** eventually starts to **run out of ink**, **producing lighter or uneven prints**
- Ribbon cartridges designed to be modular and easy to replace
- **Use** **only manufacturer approved ribbons** for compatibility's sake
- **Ribbons not re-inkable or refillable**, must be replaced one depleted
- **Ribbon replacement process**:
	1. **Power off** printer and **unplug** for safety
	2. Open **access panel** and gently remove **old ribbon cartridge** by **releasing** any **latches**
	3. Install **new ribbon** being careful to avoid touching inked surface, **ensure** it's **aligned properly**
	4. Close **Access panel** and turn **printer** back **on**
	5. Perform a **test print** to verify proper installation

### Printhead Replacement
- **Always check manufacturer's guidelines prior to replacing**
- Takes a lot of abuse, and will eventually need replacing
	- **Individual pins will eventually stop working**, causing missing lines or gaps in prints
	- **Using** a **worn printhead** **can damage** the **ribbon** **and lead to incomplete prints**
- **Take care when replacing, especially if printer used recently**
	- Printhead can get very hot
- **Some printers have screws that need to be removed**
	- **Other printers have** a **release lever or bar** to remove printhead
- Replacing both printhead and ribbon cartridge simultaneously will result in the best output possible
- **Printhead Replacement Process:**
	1. **Power off**, **unplug** the printer, and allow it to **cool down** for safety
	2. Open **access panel** and remove **old printhead** (screws, clips, or latches)
		- Remove **ribbon** if needed to access printhead
	3. Install **new printhead**, ensuring proper compatibility and **secure it** (screws, clips, or latches)
		- Compatibility can be checked via the printhead's part number
	4. Reinstall **Ribbon** (if applicable)
	5. Close **access panel** and **power** printer **on**
	6. Perform a **test print** to verify proper install

### Replacing Paper
- Tractor **paper must feed perfectly into tractor holes** on either side
- **Forms need to be positioned correctly**, printer will print on spaces of the **form** and thus **needs to fit a predefined space**
- Uses extremely long, continuous piece of paper instead of individual pages
	- **Paper must feed without constraint** (nothing in the way)
- Misaligned paper can cause jams and misfeeds
- **Paper replacement process**:
	1. **Power off** printer
	2. Open **paper compartment**
	3. Release **plastic latches** and **remove** any **remaining paper**
	4. Load **new paper**, **aligning** **paper** **holes** **with tractor feed spikes**
	5. **Secure paper** **by** lowering the **latches** to hold it in place
	6. **Feed** a **small portion** **of paper** through **to check alignment**
	7. Close **paper compartment** and **power on**
	8. Perform **test print** to verify proper install
