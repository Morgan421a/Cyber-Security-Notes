### Cleaning Print Heads
- **Print heads have small holes** (nozzles) in them **to spray small droplets of ink onto a page**
- **Print heads difficult to keep clean**
- **Check with manufacturer for best practices** to keep print head clean
- Many printers run an **automated cleaning process daily**
	- Prevents clogged heads <- big issue
	- **Colour streaks** or **sections of missing colour** **likely** due to **ink remaining on print heads**
- **Cleaning process can be started manually**
	- Takes a few minutes
	- May need to be cleaned more often depending on rate of usage
- **Some print heads or cartridges can be removed for manual cleaning**
	- **Care must be taken when cleaning by hand**

### Replacing Inkjet Cartridges
- Ink cartridges usually separate colours or combined into a single cartridge
- Easy to replace
	- Remove old cartridge out of holder, replace, and allow printer to calibrate
		- **Calibration ensures cartridges aligned properly on** the **output**
- Empty cartridges typically recyclable
	- Due to all plastic construction

### Inkjet Printer Calibration
- **Ensures nozzles align properly to** the **paper**
	- **Lines should be crisp** and **colours should align**
- **Calibration process usually automated**
	- **Minor adjustments can be made manually** to further accuracy of results

### Clearing Jams
- Paper will eventually get stuck on components during print process
- Open side of printer where jam indicated
	- Remove any paper from the path with **firm pressure to prevent tearing**
- Check for any paper scraps or loose paper before attempting to print again
