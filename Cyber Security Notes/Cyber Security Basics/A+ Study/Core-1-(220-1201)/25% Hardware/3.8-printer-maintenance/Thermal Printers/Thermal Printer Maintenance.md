### Thermal Paper Replacement
- **Thermal paper relatively cheap** but **impossible to substitute**
	- Thermal paper designed to work with thermal printers exclusively
	- **Incorrect/incompatible type** may **reduce print quality** or **cause paper jams** and **potential printer damage**
- **Many different sizes**, unlike laser printer papers
	- Paper should be designed for make and model of printer being used
- Most Thermal paper **chemical coating only on one side**
	- If pages printing blank, paper likely inserted wrong way
- **Store paper in cool, dark place to prevent premature fading**
- Simple replacement process
	1. Pop open top of printer
	2. Remove old roll
	3. Place in new roll, ensuring the correct orientation, **heat sensitive side should face printhead**
		- If unsure, scratch small area with fingernail, side that turns black is the chemical side
	4. Align guides (if applicable) to prevents misfeeds and skewed prints
	5. Close printer and ensure it's locked in place
	6. Perform a test print

### Paper Sensitivity to Heat
- Heat used to create image on thermal paper
	- **More heat from another source will darken the entire page**
- **Avoid hot areas**, such as
	- Car dashboard, Radiator, hair dryers, etc
- **Pages** will often **fade overtime**
	- **Don't use as archival media**
		- **Additional copies** printed **on** a **different** **type of paper** can be **archived safely** (paper dependent)
		- **Digital Copies** of page **can be** taken (e.g. a photo) to keep an **archived** version without needing to print another

### Cleaning the Heating Element
- **Check Manufacturer's recommendations prior to cleaning or applying any solution**
- **Commonly** cleaned using **isopropyl alcohol (IPA)**
	- **IPA cleaning pens** can be used to make **cleaning process easier** and **more accurate**
- **Swab heating element gently** to **prevent risk of damaging surrounding components**
- **Cleaning cards** can also be used
	- Sent through printer instead of paper
	- **Cleans** the **head** **and paper pathways** of the printer
- **Cleaning printhead prevents streaking, faded prints, missing lines, and ensures consistent print quality**
- **Cleaning Steps**:
	1. Power off printer and allow to cool down
	2. Use appropriate cleaning materials in-line with manufacturer recommendations
	3. Clean gently to prevent risk of damage through excessive pressure
	4. Let printhead dry fully before powering back on and resuming use

### Removing Debris
- **Overtime printers** will **build up paper bits and dust**
	- Can **cause jams** and **reduce print efficiency**
- **More portable thermal printers** can be **taken outside** and have **air blown through** the **internals** to remove the debris
	- Use **compressed air** in **short bursts**
- **Thermal printers that can't be moved** so easily can be **wiped out with** a **damp cloth**
- **Avoid using a vacuum**
	- Using a **vacuum with dust** may **generate static electricity**
	- **Vacuums designed specifically for computers** can be used
		- **Resist static buildup/discharge**

### Calibrating the Printer
- Use **calibration routines** to **align paper feed and ensure clear prints**
- Perform **regular tests** to **verify DPI and print quality**
