### Motherboard Form Factors
- **Form factor** = The shape, layout and type of case and power supply compatible with a motherboard
	- Determines type and number of components that can be installed <- the foundation for building a custom PC
	- **Influences** the **size and functionality of** the **computer**
- **Physical Size**
	- Needs to fit the case
- **Layout of components** on the motherboard
	- Some motherboards may have space for small changes
- **Power**
	- Most motherboards have standard power connections, but some motherboards may have slightly different connections
- **Airflow**
	- Cooling system needs to be able to keep up with the heat generated by the motherboard
- Over 40 different types of motherboards <- Only 3 needed for Comptia A+
	- ATX <- Largest of the three
	- microATX <- Middle size of the three, similar layout to standard ATX
	- ITX <- Smallest of the three

### ATX Form Factor
- **Advanced Technology Extended**
- Standardised by Intel in 1995
	- Has seen many updates over the years
- **12" x 9.6"** (304 mm x 244 mm)
- **Up to 7 expansion slots**
- **Rear Port cluster** for integrated audio, video, networking, etc
- **Power**
	- **20 pin** connector <- Used by **early versions** for **main system board power**
	- **24 pin** connector, additional 4/8 pin connector <- Used by **more modern versions** for **main system board power**

### microATX (uATX, M-ATX)
- **Smaller than standard ATX** motherboard but **similar in layout**
	- Limited expansion slots (less space for GPUs, Sound cards, NICs, etc)
- **9.6" x 9.6"** (244 mm x 244 mm) <- Smaller, square board
- **Fewer Expansion slots (up to 4)**
- Includes **rear port cluster and expansion slots**
- **Backward compatibility**
	- Similar mounting points to standard ATX
	- Similar power connectors to standard ATX
- **Very popular form factor** <- due to following same standards as the standard ATX while fitting into a smaller form factor

### ITX Form Factor
- A **series of low-power motherboards**
	- Small form factor
- **Mini-ITX screw-compatible with ATX** <- Same screw points as ATX
	- Fits almost any enclosure (case) <- Compatible with ATX Cases
	- **6.7" x 6.7"** (170 mm x 170 mm)
	- **One expansion slot**
	- **Rear port cluster**
- Small form factor uses:
	- Fitting into tight areas
	- single-purpose computing i.e. streaming media
