### 24-pin Motherboard Power
- **Main motherboard power**
	- Provides **+3.3 V, +/-5 V and +/-12 V DC power** to the motherboard and the components connected to it
- 20 Pin was the original ATX standard
	- **24 pin added for PCIe power**
- **Possible** to connect a **24-pin connector to** a **20-pin motherboard** <- Some cables use a **20-pin + 4-pin connector**

### PCIe 6-pin and 8-pin Power
- Used to **provide additional power for PCIe** adaptors
	- Provides **+12 V**
	- 6-pin (75 watts)
	- 8-pin (150 watts)
- **Some cables** have **2 pieces** <- **6-pin and 2-pin** = 8-pin, can be used on both 6-pin and 8-pin connectors
- Commonly used on high-power components such as video cards (GPU)

### Storage Drive Interfaces - SATA
- **L-shape interfaces** to fit the **7-pin L-shape SATA connectors** for storage devices
- Provide data connectivity to the SATA drives
#### eSATA Expansion
- Usually plugs into the bus of the computer
- Has 2 eSATA interfaces on the outside of the computer

### Headers
- Sometimes called **Pin Headers**
- Simple electrical interface
- Connector attached to the header
- Many different uses, including:
	- Power
	- Peripheral Connections <- e.g. speakers
	- Lights
	- Buttons <- e.g. power buttons on the case

### M.2 Connector
- Small slot on the motherboard, used for **m.2 drives**
	- Drives often secured via screw on opposite end from m.2 slot
