- **Convert between** different **connectors** when **cables** are **electrically compatible** <- Share compatible voltage levels for logic states and managing EMI
- Convert from one format to another (e.g. need ethernet but only USB available)
- Often designed as a temporary fix
	- Sometimes do end up being a permanent connection

### DVI to HDMI
- DVI-D and HDMI = Electrically compatible
	- HDMI backwards compatible with DVI-D
	- No signal conversion required <- Both digital
	- No loss of video quality

### DVI to VGA
- **DVI-A** includes **analog signals**
	- Backwards compatible with VGA
	- Only **640 x 480** officially supported
- May only need an adapter since both use analog signals
- VGA to **DVI digital** needs a converter
	- Analog -> Digital

### USB to Ethernet
- **Some laptops lack** a **wired Ethernet Interface**
- Convert USB to Ethernet

### USB-C to USB-A
- Convert newer USB-C connector to older USB-A or vice versa

### USB Hub
- Typically support multiple different outputs on the hub
	- High speed USB connectivity
- Connects to a computer/laptop typically via USB
