### Structured Cabling Standards
- **International ISO/IEC 11801** Cabling Standards
	- Defines classes of networking standards
- **Telecommunications Industry Association (TIA)**
	- US Based standards organisation focused on:
		- Standards, market analysis, trade shows, government affairs, etc.
	- **Created a standard for the installation of cables** in a commercial building
		- **ANSI/TIA-568**: Commercial Building Telecommunications Cabling Standard
	- Commonly reference for pin and pair assignments of 8-conductor 100-ohm balanced twisted pair cabling (e.g. ethernet cables)
		- **T568A** and **T568B**
- Ensures compatibility and proper data transmission between network devices

### T568A and T568B Termination
- T568A and T568B are **different pin assignments** for **8 positions, 8 contacts (8P8C)** connectors such as RJ-45
	- Colours between both standards is slightly different
	- Used to create **Straight-Through** and **Crossover** cables for Ethernet Networks
- **T568A**:
	- White Green, Green, White Orange, Blue, White Blue, Orange, White Brown, Brown
	- Common in government installations or mandated contracts
- **T568B**:
	- White Orange, Orange, White Green, Blue, White Blue, Green, White Brown, Brown
- **T568B Most commonly used**
	- Difficult and unusual to start with one standards and change mid stream
- **Shouldn't terminate one end of a cable with opposite standards**
	- Both ends should use the same standard
	- **Never been the definition of a Gigabit Ethernet Crossover Cable**
- Some jacks have the colours and standards denoted on them
	- Some may not seemingly follow the standards but in actual fact will put the cables in the correct order for the chosen standards into the RJ-45 connector
