### ST - Straight Tip
- **Bayonet Connector** <- **Push in** and **twist to lock** in place
	- Untwist to remove
 ![[Pasted image 20260901160220.png|267]]

### SC - Subscriber Connector
- Sometimes called **Standard Connector** or **Square Connector**
- Pushes on to lock
	- Pull outside of connector to unlock
- **Popular fibre connector**
	- Common in a lot of data centres
- Can be used as **individual** fibres **or** **combined** as a single unit with a **pair of fibre** inside
	- Easy to **connect** and **disconnect** **send and receive side** **simultaneously**
![[Pasted image 20260901160444.png|288]]

### LC - Lucent Connector
- Sometimes called **Local Connector** or **Little Connector**
- Smaller and more compact
- Locks in place with a clip
- **Popular fibre connector**
- Can be used as **individual** fibres **or** **combined** as a single unit with a **pair of fibre** inside
	- Easy to **connect** and **disconnect** **send and receive side** **simultaneously**
![[Pasted image 20260901160643.png|295]]

### MTRJ - Mechanical Transfer Register Jack
- Compact design
	- Transmit and receive pins in a single connector
	- Half the size of SC, ST, or LC connectors
- Allows for high-density fibre port configurations
