- **Caused by**:
	- Device not made discoverable or powered on
	- Incorrect PIN or pairing failure
	- Low battery on Bluetooth devices
	- Distance exceeding recommended range
- **Symptoms**:
	- Unable to detect or pair with devices
	- Frequent disconnections
	- Laggy or delayed responses in paired devices
- **Troubleshooting**:
	1. Ensure Bluetooth pairing is enabled on both devices and that they're in pairing mode
	2. Check if device needs a PIN for pairing
	3. Charge Bluetooth device
	4. Stay within recommended range (10 M/ 30 feet)
	5. Remove and re-pair device to reset any pairing issues
	6. Check for driver updates on laptops and tablets
	7. Turn of airplane mode, which may disable bluetooth

- Use Bluetooth devices within short-range environments
- Keep devices updated <- Maintains compatibility with new Bluetooth standards
- Monitor battery levels to avoid connectivity issues
