- **Caused by**: 
	- Faulty LED backlight on modern devices
	- Malfunctioning Cold Cathode Fluorescent Lamp (CCFL) and inverter failure on older devices
- **Symptoms**:
	- Screen appears faint or difficult to read
	- Content barely visible, even with brightness increased
- **Troubleshooting**:
	1. Check Power supply to backlight
	2. Inspect inverter for issues (CCFL based displays)
	3. Replace backlight if confirmed defective
