- **Caused by**:
	- Physical damage such as drops, or liquid exposure
	- Poorly installed screen protector or debris
	- Loose or disconnected digitiser cable
- **Symptoms**:
	- Touchscreen completely black
	- Screen not responding to input though is visually intact
	- Inconsistent touch response
	- No reaction to touch inputs
- **Troubleshooting**:
	1. Remove any screen protector and clean the surface
	2. Reset device <- Resolves software related issues
	3. Inspect internal connections if screen was recently replaced

- *Mobile Phone Reset:
	- Apple iOS reset, Two common methods:
		- Press power button, slide to power off, press power button
		- Hold down power button and another button (e.g. home or volume) for 10 seconds <- Forces device reset
	- Android restart, has various methods:
		- Remove battery, put back in, power on
		- Hold down power and volume until restart
			- Key combination varies between phones
			- Some phones don't have key-based reset
	- Check device documentation to find out reset method for phone
