### Liquid Damage
- Water or other liquid makes contact with internal components of a device
	- Can be caused in high humidity environments due to condensation within the device
- Many phones have a **Liquid Contact Indicator** (LCI)
	- LCI Changes colour when its been near liquid
	- One of the first things checked when phone sent in for a repair
- Symptoms:
	- Device not turning on or acting erratically
	- Screen discoloration or flickering
	- Corrosion on internal components
	- Inconsistent charging or battery swelling
- Steps to take:
	1. Immediately power phone off, don't turn it back on <- Prevents short circuits and further damage
	2. Dry external Surface
	3. Remove case, cards, back, and battery if possible <- Try to allow as much airflow through the phone as possible
	4. Disassemble device if possible <- carefully dry internal components
	5. Use Isopropyl alcohol (IPA) <- Clean affected circuit boards to prevent corrosion
	6. Replace battery if any signs of damage <- Batteries very sensitive to liquid exposure
	- Leave phone while it dries, DO NOT:
		- Turn it on, Charge it, Connect it to power, Press any keys, Heat the phone, Move it around
	- Wait at least a day, or until phone appears dry, and then wait a few hours after that
	- Once phone appears dry and grace period given, try powering on
		- If process didn't work properly phone may need to be serviced
- Prevent by using waterproof cases, keeping devices away from water prone areas, and educating users on device handling in wet environments
