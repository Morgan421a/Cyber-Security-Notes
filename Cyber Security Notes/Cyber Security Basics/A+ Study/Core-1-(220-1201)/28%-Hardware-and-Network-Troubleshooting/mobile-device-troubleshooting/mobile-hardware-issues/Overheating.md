### Overheating
- Occurs when mobile device can't dissipate heat properly <- Potentially causes component damage
	- Phone will automatically shut down to avoid overheating damage
- **Caused by**:
	- Blocking cooling vents (e.g. using laptop on soft surface such as a bed)
	- Exposure to high temps (e.g. leaving device in a car)
	- Dust accumulation in cooling fans
	- Intensive tasks causing excessive heat generation
		- Phone will heat up faster during charging/discharging the battery, CPU usage, and display light
			- Check app usage, some can use a lot of CPU
- **Symptoms**:
	- Sudden device shutdown due to high temps
	- Slow or degraded performance or response times
	- Fans running at a high speed constantly
	- Shortened battery life
- **If device has or is overheating**:
	- Allow device to cool before continuing use
	- Ensure vents aren't obstructed
	- Consider replacing thermal paste or internal cooling components if issue persists
- **To prevent overheating**:
- Avoid direct sunlight <- Makes phone overheat quickly
- Avoid leaving device in hot environments
- Use laptops on hard, flat surfaces <- Allows for proper airflow
- Use cooling pads for extended laptop use <- Improve airflow
- Regularly clean cooling fans and vents
