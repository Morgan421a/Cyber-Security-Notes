### White Stripes
- Horizontal blank lines across printed page
- **Commonly caused by**:
	- Dirty or faulty Photosensitive drum (Laser printers)
	- Uneven toner distribution
	- Clogged printhead jets (inkjet printers)
	- Corona wire may be dirty preventing proper drum charging
- **Solutions**:
	- Clean or replace photosensitive drum (Laser printers)
	- Gently rock toner cartridge to evenly distribute toner
	- Carry out printhead cleaning cycle (Inkjet Printers)
	- Clean corona wire

### Black Stripes or Entirely Black Pages
- **Commonly Caused by**:
	- Dirty or malfunctioning primary charge roller (Laser printers)
	- High-voltage power supply issues
- **Solutions**:
	- Check and clean corona wire or charge roller (Laser Printers)
	- Replace drum and toner cartridge if necessary

### Vertical or Horizontal Lines
- Lines running down or across the page
- Commonly Caused by:
	- Dirty feed rollers
	- Damaged or worn drum unit (Laser Printers)
- Solutions:
	- Thoroughly clean feed rollers
	- Inspect and replace photosensitive drum if needed (Laser Printers)
