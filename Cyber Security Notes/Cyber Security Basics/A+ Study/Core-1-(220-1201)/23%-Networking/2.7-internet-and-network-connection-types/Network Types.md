### LAN
- **Local Area Network (LAN)**
	- Local is relative
- Typically referred to as:
	- **A group of networks close to a specific location**
		- e.g. a building or group of buildings
- Often have high connection speeds
	- Networks which are further away, likely not on the LAN and will typically have slower speeds
- **Distance:**
	- Up to **100 metres** (300 feet) for **Cat 5 cabling**, **extendable** with **fibre optics**
- Use **Ethernet** (**802.3**) or **Wi-Fi** (**802.11**) wireless

### WAN
- **Wide Area Network (WAN)**
- Network connecting **geographically dispersed** **networks** over a **large area**
- Distance:
	- **Spans the globe**
		- Can be across cities, countries, or globally
- Usually **connects LANs across a distance**
	- Often much **slower** than the LAN due to the larger distance
- Many **different technologies** used to communicate over a **WAN** for example:
	- **Point-to-point serial**
	- **Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS)**
	- **Terrestrial** <- Communication facilities **on Earth**
	- **Non-terrestrial** <- Communication facilities **not on Earth** such as Satellites
	- **VPNs**
	- Dedicated **Leased Lines**

### PAN
- **Personal Area Network (PAN)**
- A user's private network
	- e.g. **Bluetooth**, Infrared (**IR**), Near Field Communication (**NFC**)
- Used in many different areas for various purposes, such as: 
	- **In Cars**:
		- To provide **audio output** from a **phone** for tasks such as **hands-free** communication
		- Integrating with media services installed on a phone (e.g. Spotify)
	- **Mobile devices**:
		- Connect to **wireless devices** such as a **headset** or **speaker**
	- **Health related technology**:
		- Collecting health details on a smartphone
		- Workout telemetry
		- Daily reports

### MAN
- **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)**
- Network connecting multiple LANs within a city
- **Larger** than a **LAN**, usually **smaller** than a **WAN**
	- Up to **25 Miles** or more
- Typically sits in a **single geographic area** such as a city
	- e.g. connect to remote locations around a city
- Traditionally **proprietary** networks that used different technologies as a backbone, such as:
	- **Asynchronous Transfer Mode (ATM)**
	- **Switched Multimegabit Data Service (SMDS)**
	- **Fibre Distributed Data Interface (FDDI)**
- Becoming **increasingly standardised** on **Ethernet**, such as:
	- **Metro Ethernet** = A type of Ethernet connectivity in a local city
- Common to see government ownership
	- Putting their own **fibre** into the ground since they **"own"** the **right-of-way** to put anything they want into the pipes
		- Done to create their own **MAN** using their own fibre

### SAN
- **Storage Area Network (SAN)**
- **Network** that provides **access** to **configurable storage pools**
- A repository of storage used by organisations
- Focused on providing high speed storage access using technologies such as **iSCSI** or **fibre channels**
	- **iSCSI** (Internet Small Computer System Interface) = **IP-based storage networking protocol**; enables **block-level access** to remote storage devices over a **TCP/IP network**
- Looks and feels like a local storage device
- Typically a **High speed** connection
	- Due to **large amounts of data** being **transferred** to and from the **SAN**
	- Very **efficient** at **reading** and **writing**
- Uses **Block-Level Access** = **Reading** and **Writing** data in **fixed-sized chunks** (**blocks**) rather than as whole files
	- Allows for **high-performance**, **low-latency**
- **Requires a lot of bandwidth**
	- Often use an **isolated network** and **high-speed network technologies**

### WLAN
- **Wireless Local Area Network/Wireless LAN (WLAN)**
- **Wireless** **network** connecting devices within a limited area **using** **Wi-Fi (802.11)**
- Allow devices to move around a local area while maintaining network connection
- **Access Points** can be used to **expand** the **coverage** of a **WLAN**

### CAN
- **Campus Area Network (CAN)**
- **Network** that connects **multiple LANs** within a **limited geographical area**
- **Larger** than a **LAN**, **smaller** than a **MAN**
	- Typically spans 1,000 Metres to 5,000 Metres
- **Faster** and more **reliable** than **larger networks** (e.g. MAN, WAN)
	- **Slower** than **LAN**
- Use **fibre optic backbone** cables to link buildings
- Networking equipment typically all owned by the organisation or institution that owns the campus
- Typically integrates both **Ethernet** (802.3) and **Wi-Fi** (802.11) connectivity
	- Supports high density user access and resource sharing
