- **Google Android**
	- Open-source OS, based on Linux
	- **Maintained by** a group of companies called the **Open Handset Alliance**
	- Supported on many different manufacturer's and their many types of hardware
#### Android Apps
- Apps designed for Android systems can be developed using Windows, macOS, and Linux with the **Android SDK** (Software Developer Kit)
- Apps are available from **Google Play Store**
	- **Some apps** are also **available through third-party sites** (i.e. Amazon Appstore)
