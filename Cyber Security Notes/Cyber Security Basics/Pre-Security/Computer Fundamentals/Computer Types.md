**A Few Different Types of Computers**
- **Laptop** = Portable - Used for portable every day computing, struggle with larger/more complex tasks
- **Desktop** = Fixed - Used for more intensive everyday tasks, more powerful than a laptop, better where sustained and consistent performance is needed
- **Workstation** = Fixed - Similar to desktop but uses specialised components to prioritise accuracy and reliability by reducing errors during long/complex computations
- **Servers** = Fixed - Run continuously responding to requests from multiple clients at the same time.
- **Smartphones** = Portable - Pocket sized, optimised for battery life and connectivity
- **Tablet** = Portable - Similar to smartphones but with a larger screen, tend to be more powerful
- **IoT Device** = Device dependent - Network connected device with a single purpose i.e. fitness tracker, thermostat, smart door bell
- **Embedded Computer** - Computer built into another device typically not connected to a network unlike IoT (though it can be connected, just not to the internet) i.e. Coffee machine controller, automatic door sensor, lamp dimmer chip

**Why so Many Types?**
- Every Device has its trade offs, for example:
	- Laptops are smaller and portable but therefore offer worse performance than a desktop and are also more prone to overheating since they have a smaller cooling system in comparison: smaller fans, heat pipes and heat sink. 
	- Desktops on the other hand: Large fans, more airflow space, tower cooler but they're fixed and require more power to use
	- Servers and critical system use redundancies such as extra Power supplies and disks to avoid failure, improving uptime, but they're also fixed and farm more difficult to move and setup than a desktop


## **Summary**
- Many different types of computer exist, some are:
	- **Laptop** - Portable, though less powerful and prone to overheating 
		- Good for working anywhere 
		- Not great for more intensive tasks
	- **Desktop Computer** - Fixed, more powerful than laptops but need to be plugged in during use, 
		- Have a user interface in the form of a monitor unlike a server
		- Good when needing consistency for a task or carrying out more intensive tasks that a laptop may struggle with
		- Performance heavily depends on its hardware components, better performance = more expensive
	- **Smartphone** - Small, portable, optimised for battery life and performance, 
		- Good for day to day use 
		- Not great for consistent performance or intensive tasks
	- **IoT Device** - Built into devices that have a single purpose, allows them to connect to the internet in order to send and receive data
		- Used for devices such as smart doorbells and thermostats
	- **Server** - Fixed, typically used to store data, handle and respond to client requests or host apps and services. 
		- Lack a user interface and must be plugged in
		- Good when large amounts of traffic need to be handled in a consistent manner
		- Often use fallback servers as a failover in case one runs into issues
	- **Workstation** - Fixed, more powerful version of a desktop computer that uses more hardware to give it the extra power 
		- Used for more intensive tasks or where more accuracy is required such as 3D modelling/rendering
		- Usually quite expensive and take longer to setup
	- **Tablet** - Mobile, slightly larger version of a smartphone
		- Tend to be more powerful than a smartphone 
		- Not great for intensive tasks or those where consistent performance is needed
	- **Embedded Computer** -Built in to a device, such as a coffee machine, to run a script (piece of code) in order to fulfil a purpose
		- Not to be confused with IoT devices 
		- Typically not connected to a network

- So many types of computers exist because they all come with their own trade offs such as:
	- Laptops tend to provide less performance than a standard desktop computer and due to their smaller cooling system they are more prone to overheating. However their small size makes them good for working on the go and may be better for tasks where the user doesn't stay in one place long enough to warrant a desktop
	- Phones may be less powerful than a laptop and unable to carry out certain tasks a laptop can but they're often more efficient with regards to battery life and make communication easier through the use of apps and mobile data allowing users to access the internet anywhere that service exists
