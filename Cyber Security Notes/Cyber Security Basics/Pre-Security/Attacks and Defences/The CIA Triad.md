#### **Understanding the CIA Triad**
- **CIA**:
	- **Confidentiality** - Ensures sensitive data can only be accessed by authorised individuals
		- If not maintained, unauthorised individuals can access the data which may result in financial loss, privacy violations, or legal consequences
		- Maintained through different processes such as encryption and access controls
		
	- **Integrity** - Ensures unauthorised individuals do not modify data
		- Without, data can be altered and no longer trusted; unauthorised changes to data can potentially lead to dangerous consequences
		- Maintained through various techniques such as file permissions (Principal of Least Privilege)
		
	- **Availability** - Ensures data and services are available to authorised users when needed
		- Modern businesses rely heavily on their digital services; if they become unavailable, there is no more business, causing them a huge loss. <- Even short periods of downtime can have serious consequences on the business and users.
		- Maintained through measures such as load balancers and firewalls (in the case of a website/web server)

#### **The Security Mindset**
- When a security incident occurs, it's often explained in terms of what was affected, generally done by asking questions such as:
	- Was sensitive data exposed to unauthorised individuals?
	- Was data being modified without permission?
	- Were systems or services unavailable to users when they needed?
