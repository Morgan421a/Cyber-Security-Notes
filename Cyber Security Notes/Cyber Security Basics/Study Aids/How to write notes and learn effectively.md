
- Don't just copy exactly, re-word it and shorten it
	- Read sentence/paragraph or do an activity
	- Hide page for 30 seconds
	- Try to write from memory what was just read/learned
	- Check notes against sentence/paragraph to make adjustments if needed

- abbreviate and use symbols to help shorten notes further

- Use the car park method when seeing new words for the first time:
	- Store questions to research later
	- Write the word at the top of the note in a "car park" list
	- Highlight the word within the note and move on
	- Only research the terms in the car park if they directly block ability or progress to completing the room
	- Review the car parks during low energy study sessions (e.g. After an early shift or after a bad night's sleep where energy is low).

- Use spaced repetition for topics:
	- Day 2/3 after learning - Write a summary for the topic from memory, only checking notes once finished

- Late shift or no shift days:
	- Medication around 8 or 9 am, focus should peak around the 10 am - 13 pm window for studying
	- Use time to deal with most difficult conceptual topics
	- Carry out labbing, only use obsidian to paste specific command syntax, keep notes atomic and command-focused like a tool box
